### 👋 A Little About Me

Hey, I'm **Pedro**! I'm an AI Engineer and Data Scientist based in Lisbon, Portugal.

I enjoy working where **scientific curiosity meets engineering** — turning complex ideas into AI systems, useful tools, and products people can actually use.

My background is in **Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, and Epidemiology**, and I'm preparing to begin a **PhD in AI for Medicine at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Lisbon (FMUL)**.

Professionally, I work on data science, analytics, and AI-driven automation in the public sector.

But I don't want to stop at using AI. I want to **build the tools, architectures, and experiences that make it more useful.**

- 🧠 **Research interests:** Synthetic data, data quality, LLM evaluation, and AI for medicine.
- 🤖 **Engineering focus:** Agentic AI, RAG, LLM systems, orchestration, and evaluation.
- 🚀 **Builder mindset:** Open-source tools, developer platforms, and experimental AI products.
- 🧬 **Scientific roots:** Bioinformatics, computational biology, and epidemiology.
- 📍 **Based in:** Lisbon, Portugal.

### 🚀 Featured Open-Source Projects

#### 🏛️ [MesoDeities](https://github.com/PedroSAP/MesoDeities)
An LLM-powered RAG application for exploring Mesopotamian mythology and deities.

`Python` · `LlamaIndex` · `FAISS` · `FastAPI` · `Hugging Face`

#### 📜 [NobrezaPortuguesaQA](https://github.com/PedroSAP/NobrezaPortuguesaQA)

`Python` · `RAG` · `Embeddings` · `LlamaIndex` · `FastAPI`

#### 🌱 [PlantsScientificGenerator](https://github.com/PedroSAP/PlantsScientificGenerator)

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Hugging Face`
